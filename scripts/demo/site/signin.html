What to check

  1. Click the email field (or the key at its right). The list shows 4 usernames and a search field.
  2. sam@example.com is one row, "3 sites". Its chevron (or →) shows mail.… with its own, different password.
  3. Pick the mail.… one: it fills that site's password (the field shows its length: 18 dots).
  4. family@example.com shows its saved title "Family account".
  5. Type work in the email field: only sam.work@ is left. Type nobody@x: the list closes.
  6. Type in the search field: it searches usernames, sites and titles ("mail", "family").
  7. Click the key twice: the second click closes the list. The × and Escape close it too.
  8. Keys: ↑ ↓ move, Return fills, ← → close or open a group, Tab closes.
  9. Type a new email and password by hand and submit: a save card asks first.
