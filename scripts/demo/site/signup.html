What to check

  1. Type an email such as new@example.com.
  2. Click the new password field: Use Strong Password at the top. Choose it: both password fields fill.
  3. Nothing is saved yet: Settings ▸ Passwords has no new@example.com.
  4. Press Create account: the card says "Save strong password?" with new@example.com filled in, and stays until you answer.
  5. Try it again with the email left empty: the card asks for an email or username.
