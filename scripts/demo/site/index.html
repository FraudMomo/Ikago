Before you start

  • Sample logins are saved for accounts / mail.ikago-demo.localhost and blog-demo.localhost. One address and one test card are saved too.
  • In this demo, filling a password doesn't ask for Touch ID. The real app always does.
  • Each page lists what to check. Quit this window's app (⌘Q) to throw everything away.
